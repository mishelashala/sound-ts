# Logo options (soundness)

1. **Shield check** (`01-shield-check.svg`) — Small shield with a check; verified / protected entry.
2. **Seal lock** (`02-seal-lock.svg`) — Filled disc with lock notch / keyhole; opaque brands that stay sealed.
3. **Gate** (`03-gate.svg`) — Doorway / bracket with a check; checked entry only.
4. **Stamp** (`04-stamp.svg`) — Solid seal with an S mark; sound-ts stamp of soundness.
5. **Split mark** (`05-split-mark.svg`) — Two halves closing into one; unsound gap → closed whole.
